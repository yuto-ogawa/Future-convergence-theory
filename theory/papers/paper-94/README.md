### Abstract


Future Convergence Theory (FCT): An Ontological Framework for Projection, Constraint, and Manifestation

Future Convergence Theory (FCT) proposes an ontological framework in which physical reality is interpreted as the manifestation of conserved structures through ontological constraints and projection, rather than as the primary foundation of existence. The theory introduces a hierarchical sequence consisting of an Undifferentiated Preservation Structure, Ontological Constraint, Reachable Domain, Future Information Field, Quantum Allowance Layer, Gravity, Projection, and Manifested Reality.

Within this framework, possibility is not treated as a primitive assumption but as the reachable domain remaining after ontological constraints eliminate impossible regions. Future Information Field is defined as the image of the ontological constraint operator, while the Quantum Allowance Layer represents the residual unconstrained degrees of freedom required for manifestation. Gravity is interpreted as a projection-maintaining mechanism rather than a fundamental interaction.

The mathematical formulation introduces preservation tensor spaces, self-adjoint ontological constraint operators, a variational action principle, and field equations governing preservation tensors, constraint connections, and the Quantum Allowance Layer. Existing physical theories, including General Relativity, the Schrödinger equation, and Yang–Mills gauge theory, are positioned as effective low-constraint or post-projection limits of the proposed framework rather than independent foundations.

The appendices further discuss theoretical predictions for cosmology and particle physics, including inflation, baryon asymmetry, CP symmetry violation, and neutrino mass, together with their potential observational consequences. Finally, the framework is extended to semantic density tensors, premise update systems, artificial intelligence, language, ethics, and civilization through the Λ-System, providing a unified ontological interpretation across physical and cognitive domains.

This work presents Future Convergence Theory as a unified ontological and mathematical framework intended to provide a common structural basis for physical manifestation, information, cognition, and future theoretical development.
